Voxel downsampling with an octree

How downsample() bins points into voxels with integer floor division and averages each cell in one vectorised pass.

Every point gets an integer voxel_key: its coordinates divided by the voxel size and floored. Points that share a key fall in the same cube, so grouping by key is the whole spatial index — no tree is built.

Note the second point: -0.2 lands in cell -1, not 0. Rounding toward zero would merge two different cubes around the origin.
